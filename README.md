# Seoul-Bike-EDA
Bike rental demand is strongly influenced by time, weather, and seasonal factors.
Peak usage occurs during morning and evening hours, reflecting daily commuting
patterns.
Summer and favorable weather conditions (high visibility, moderate temperature)
lead to higher rentals.
Adverse weather conditions such as rainfall, snowfall, and high humidity reduce bike
usage.
Bike services remain consistently operational, ensuring reliability across all days.
In my opinion, understanding these patterns helps in optimizing bike availability,
improving planning, and enhancing overall service efficiency.
